A calmer way to compute.

Digital Mind is not another app to distract you. It is a personal operating layer designed to reduce mental load, remember what matters, and help you act with clarity.

Memory

Store notes, events, conversations, relationships, ideas, and lessons as living context.

Agents

Small AI helpers that research, draft, organize, summarize, and prepare work for approval.

Approval

Nothing important happens blindly. Your agents prepare the work. You stay in control.

Ambient Life

Briefings, reminders, focus support, and gentle guidance so you can spend less time staring at screens.

“Digital Mind is a personal, open-source intelligence layer that helps humans remember, decide, create, and live more freely.”

Open-source by design.

Digital Mind should be local-first, privacy-respecting, portable, and repairable. It can begin as a PWA, grow into a local agent system, and eventually become part of TommyOS and the larger 3dvr.tech ecosystem.

PWA

Works on phone, tablet, desktop, and eventually wearable systems.

Local-first

Use local storage, local models, Termux, Linux, and optional cloud sync.

Human-first

Dark mode, low stimulation, gentle design, and tools that support the nervous system.
